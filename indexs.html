About us

The taste of the frontier, in Gulberg

Shinwari cooking is simple and confident: good meat, a heavy karahi, salt, black pepper, tomatoes and a hot flame. At Golden Shinwari we cook it that way for families, friends and late-night crowds at Main Market.

Dine-inTakeawayFamily seatingBulk & party orders

How we make a Shinwari karahi

  1. Fresh cuts, every dayChicken, mutton and dumba prepared in the kitchen each morning.
  2. Few spices, done rightSalt, black pepper, green chilli and tomato. Nothing to hide behind.
  3. Straight from the flameServed bubbling in the karahi with hot naan.
